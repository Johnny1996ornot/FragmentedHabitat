# Fragmented Habitat

**Fragmented Habitat** is a game of survival, competition, and risky journeys across a divided landscape. Players guide animal families from their habitat to a resource patch, collect food, and bring it home. Roads and roaming predators threaten each crossing, while rival animals can challenge occupied spaces and push one another into danger.

Food helps you win, revive animals, and produce offspring. Every decision involves a trade-off: protect your food supply, expand your family, or take a risk for a better position.

**Players:** 1–7, sharing one screen  
**Playable animals:** Rat, squirrel, raccoon, rabbit, capybara, tarsier, and beetle  
**Objective:** Be the first family to return home with at least **7 banked food**.

## 1. Starting the game

Each player chooses a different animal species and starts with:

- One animal token.
- Three banked food.
- No cards.

Banked food belongs to the whole family. Food collected during a journey is carried by the individual animal until it returns home.

## 2. Taking your turn

On your turn:

1. You may produce offspring if eligible.
2. Roll one six-sided die.
3. Choose one of your living animals to move.
4. Move up to the number rolled.
5. End your movement and resolve any landing effect.

You may move **up, down, left, or right**, changing direction between steps. Diagonal movement is not allowed.

You may stop early after taking at least one step. If your selected animal has no legal move, it may pass.

Before leaving habitat, you may choose any starting row. Once you take your first step, that animal uses the rest of the turn’s movement. You cannot divide a roll between animals.

## 3. Collecting and banking food

Entering the resource patch gives your animal **2 carried food**, once per trip.

Return to habitat to add its carried food to your family’s banked total. Returning home also allows that animal to collect food on a new trip.

Carried food cannot pay for offspring or revival and does not count toward winning until banked.

## 4. Crossing the road

When an animal **ends its movement on a light gray road square**, roll a traffic die:

- **Odd:** The animal is safe.
- **Even:** The animal is hit by a car.

Passing through road squares without stopping does not require a traffic check.

The **dark gray safe haven** requires no traffic check, but another animal may contest it.

## 5. Predator movement

Predators begin **outside the board**, below their fixed lanes.

After every active player has taken a turn, each predator rolls its own die and moves that many squares:

- Predators move straight along their assigned columns.
- Their first roll brings them onto the board.
- Once inside, they reverse direction at an edge and use any remaining steps.
- Only the square where a predator finishes causes a hit.

Animals cannot voluntarily move onto a predator’s current square. A forced push onto that square causes an immediate hit.

## 6. Producing offspring

Before rolling, you may spend **2 banked food** to add an offspring at habitat.

To produce offspring:

- At least one living family animal must be at habitat.
- Your family must have fewer than three living animals.

Offspring are the same species as their family and receive no additional starting food.

A larger family gives you more animals to choose from, but you still receive only **one movement roll per turn**.

## 7. Occupied squares and contests

Only one animal may occupy an individual field square. Habitat and the resource patch are shared areas.

Your own animals block one another. Moving toward a rival’s occupied square starts a contest and uses **one movement step**.

Resolve the contest:

1. The attacker chooses **odd or even**.
2. The defender receives the opposite choice.
3. Roll one die.
4. The matching side wins.

If the attacker wins, it takes the disputed square. If the defender wins, it keeps that square.

The winner then chooses where to push the loser.

## 8. Pushing an animal

Push the loser **one square from its position before the contest**, forward, backward, or sideways.

You cannot push it into another animal’s field square.

The destination determines the effect:

- **Ordinary field:** The animal remains there.
- **Light gray road:** Immediately resolve a traffic check.
- **Safe haven:** No traffic check.
- **Predator square:** Immediately resolve a hit.
- **Off the board:** Resolve the usual revival rule, and the contest winner draws one card.

If the moving animal is hit, its turn ends. Otherwise, it may continue using its remaining movement.

An ordinary contest victory does not award a card. You must win and push the opponent **off the board**.

## 9. Reward cards

The shuffled deck contains **17 cards**:

| Card | Copies | Effect |
|---|---:|---|
| Reroll | 7 | Replace your movement die with a new roll before taking your first step. |
| Extra block | 5 | Add one movement step to your current turn. |
| Enemy backward | 3 | Move a rival’s animal one square toward habitat. |
| Safe Streets | 2 | Skip one traffic check before rolling it. |

**Reroll:** The new result replaces the original result. Extra block bonuses already played remain.

**Extra block:** You may play it even after using all your movement, provided you have not ended your turn.

**Enemy backward:** Choose an enemy on the field whose destination is unoccupied. Road and predator hazards still apply. This card does not start a contest or award another card. Moving an enemy into habitat can allow it to bank carried food.

**Safe Streets:** The owner of the animal exposed to traffic decides whether to use it. It also works after a forced road landing. It does not protect against predators.

Cards are shared by the family and discarded after use. When the draw pile runs out, shuffle the discard pile to form a new draw pile. Cards still held by players remain in their hands.

## 10. Hits, revival, and elimination

Whenever an animal is hit or pushed off the board:

- Spend **1 banked food** to revive it at habitat.
- Discard all food it was carrying.

If its family has no banked food when the hit occurs, remove that animal from the game.

The family continues while at least one animal survives. An eliminated family discards its held cards.

## 11. Winning

The first family to return an animal to habitat and reach **7 or more banked food** wins immediately.

Your starting food counts toward this total. Food spent on offspring or revival reduces your progress toward winning.

If every animal is eliminated before a family reaches the target, the game ends without a winner.
