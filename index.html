A journey through fragmented habitat

Start with 5 family food. Find fruit at the edge and return it to the forest. First to bank 7 food wins; choose a longer target in Settings.

Set up: players take turns placing 18 predators in the glowing lanes. There are three animals in each of six lanes. Click a space to place the next one, or keep your choices and fill the remaining spaces automatically.

Your turn: choose an animal and roll. Move up to that many connected spaces, inward, outward or sideways. Stop after at least one step. Before rolling you may spend 2 food to add an offspring at home, even if the others are away. Maximum three living animals.

The landscape: leave the tree-filled habitat, cross three inner green lanes, two road lanes, a safe island, two more road lanes and three outer green lanes. Resource patches are at the edge. The island is safe; movement along it goes clockwise, though you can enter and leave radially.

Traffic: stopping on any light gray road space triggers the separate sun/moon die. Moon = night, safe. Sun = day, car hit. There are three moon faces and three sun faces. Passing across the road or stopping on the dark island needs no check. Night safety is a simplified game rule.

Predators: after everyone’s turn, roll once for each green lane. All three predators in that lane move the same number clockwise. They catch animals only on their final spaces. You cannot walk onto a predator.

Harvest: green edge patches give 2 fruit; blue cornucopias give 3 and immediately trigger a hunter roll. Odd is safe; even is a hit. Collect once per trip. Return to the centre to bank fruit.

Contest: challenge an enemy’s space, choose odd or even and roll. The winner keeps that space, pushes the loser one neighbouring space, then clicks the deck to draw a card. Forced road and predator landings count. The outer edge allows off-board pushes.

Hits and cards: normally lose carried fruit and spend 1 food to revive at home. With no food, the animal leaves the game. Click ▣ beside a player to view held cards. Drawn cards unlock next round and are used manually.

  • 14 Reroll: replace movement dice before taking a step.
  • 10 Extra Block: add one movement step.
  • 6 Enemy Backward: move an enemy one space inward to a free space; landing hazards apply.
  • 4 Safe Streets: skip one sun/moon check and stay safely on the road.
  • 2 Play Dead: survive a predator in place with your fruit. Only that predator rolls again; the other two stay put.
  • 2 Angel Conservationists: revive at home free after a car or hunter hit; carried fruit is lost.

Think about it: how did the road change your route, food supply and survival? What would a wildlife bridge change? Predators belong to the ecosystem; the landscape’s barriers make the journey harder.
