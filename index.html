    How to play

    Get food. Bring it home.

    You are a family of small animals living in the forest on the left. There is plenty of fruit on the right, but a road and hungry predators stand between you and your next meal.

    Everyone starts with 3 food at home and one animal. Visit the fruit patch to pick up 2 food, then return to the forest to add it to your total. Food being carried does not count yet. The first family with 7 food at home wins, unless you choose a different goal in Settings.

    Drag the board sideways to rotate and vertically to tilt, or use the Rotate buttons. On a phone, drag with one finger and pinch with two to zoom. Trackpad pinch or scrolling over the board zooms the board; it does not zoom the website. Reset view restores the original angle without restarting the game. A tap or click on a gold square still moves your animal.

    Your turn, step by step

    1. Choose an animal and roll. Your whole family shares one roll. If you have offspring, choose which animal gets to move; you can change your choice until you take the first step.
    2. Follow the gold squares. Each neighboring square costs one step. You can go left, right, up, or down. A roll of 4 gives you up to four steps; you do not have to use them all. While still at home, you can choose any starting row for free before entering the field.
    3. Click Stop here when you are done. Finish on green land or the dark gray refuge to avoid traffic. Stop on light gray road and you must check for a car. If you have no legal move at all, you can pass.

    Passing through the road is safe from cars. Stopping on it is the risk: roll again, and an odd number means you are safe; an even number means you are hit. The dark gray strip is a refuge, so no traffic roll is needed there.

    The foxes and tigers have their own turn

    After every living family has played, roll once for each predator. They start outside the board and move straight along their marked green lanes. At an edge they turn around and use any steps they have left. A predator only catches an animal on the square where it finishes; animals it passes are safe.

    You cannot walk onto a square where a predator is standing. But another player can push you there, which causes an immediate hit. Predators never use the road.

    When two animals want the same square

    Your own animals block one another. If you try to enter another family's square, you spend one movement step and start a contest. Choose odd or even; the defender gets the other choice. Roll once. If your choice comes up, you take the square. Otherwise, the defender keeps it.

    The winner chooses a highlighted square to push the loser one step left, right, forward, or back. A push onto the road triggers traffic immediately. A push onto a predator causes a hit. You cannot push into another occupied field square. Everyone can share the forest and fruit patch.

    Win a contest. Draw a card.

    Once you win the odd/even roll, choose where to push the loser, then click the deck to draw one card. At the board's edge, an OUT square may be available. Push the loser there and they return home under the usual hit rule. Every odd/even contest winner earns one card, whether the push stays on the board or goes OUT. After the push, play pauses: the winner clicks the floating deck to reveal it and keep it in their hand. A normal push within the board also earns the winner a card. If it triggers traffic, draw first, then resolve that traffic check. If a push ends the whole game, no further card is needed.

    Wait until the next round to use a new card. If you draw in round 2, it becomes available in round 3, after all families and predators finish round 2. It stays in your hand until you click its Use button at the right time. Nothing plays automatically.

    Reroll — 7 copies: After rolling for movement, but before your first step, roll again and replace the old result. If you rolled 1, this gives you another chance; you must accept the new result. It does not give you two movement rolls.

    Extra block — 5 copies: Add one step during your movement turn. You can use it after spending your last step, as long as you have not finished your turn.

    Enemy backward — 3 copies: During your movement turn, choose a highlighted enemy and move it one square toward the forest. Its destination must be free; road and predator hazards still apply. Moving it into the forest can help it bank its food, so choose carefully! Cancel the selection to keep your card.

    Safe Streets — 2 copies: When your animal needs a traffic check, click Use before the traffic roll. That one landing is safe. This also works when someone pushes your animal onto the road, even during their turn. It does not protect against predators.

    Used cards go to the discard pile. When the deck empties, only discarded cards are reshuffled. If all 17 cards are held, there is nothing to draw until one is discarded.

    Hits and offspring

    A hit costs your family 1 food from home. Your animal revives in the forest and loses everything it was carrying. If you have no food when hit, that animal is removed. Your family keeps playing if another animal survives; if none survive, your turns are skipped and your cards are discarded.

    Before rolling, you can spend 2 food for an offspring if one of your animals is at home. You can have up to three living animals. They share food and cards, but each carries its own food. One family still gets just one movement roll per turn.

    The fruit patch gives food once per trip. You must return home before collecting again. There are no reward squares. You can undo ordinary steps before ending your turn, but rolls, contests, card use, and hits are final. If every animal is removed, the game ends with no winner.

    Use Settings to change the players, names, animals, or winning total and start a fresh game. Reset restarts the same group. Everyone shares this screen; refreshing the page starts a new two-player game.

    Make it your game.

    Change the group here. Applying settings starts a fresh game with everyone back at home.

    Start fresh?

    Keep the same players and goal. Everyone returns to the forest with 3 food, and the cards are shuffled again.
