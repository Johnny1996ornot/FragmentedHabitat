How to play

Cross the landscape, find food and make it home. Luck shapes each encounter; your routes, cards and rivals shape what happens next.

Read the board

Follow the numbers from the centre to the edge. These maps identify the landscape; the playable board divides every lane into individual spaces.

Start with no predators on the board. Light green lanes require a Stealth Check whenever an animal stops. Predators added during play remain stationary and block routes.

01 · THE MAIN GAME

Habitat Fragmentation

Gather food and come home

Start in the central habitat. Cross the green lanes and roads, collect fruit at the edge, then return home to bank it. An ordinary resource patch provides 1 fruit; a golden horn provides 3 fruit but requires a hunter check: moon means safe, sun means a hit. Each animal collects one load per trip. A harvested patch stays empty unless its fruit returns after a hit or officer escort.

Add an animal · optional

Before rolling, spend 2 banked food to add an animal at home. Its parent can be anywhere. Keep at most three living animals; move only one per turn.

Win Classic

  • Reach the chosen banked-food target first: 10 by default.
  • Be the only player with animals remaining. Resolve the complete officer patrol before declaring a last-survivor winner.
  • If all resource patches are empty, finish pending checks, then compare banked food plus carried fruit. Ties share the win.

Local extinction

If no animals remain, nobody wins Classic. A fresh full Colonization game begins: one animal, five food and four new cards each. Old totals, predators and cards reset.

02 · SETTLEMENT AND OPTIONAL OPENER

Habitat Colonization

Travel from separate edge habitats to the shared central habitat. Arriving animals stay as settlers; there is no return trip, food collection, hunter or golden harvest.

Settlement and recruitment

First to 6 settlers wins. If the habitat reaches its carrying capacity first (12 by default), most settlers wins; ties share the win. Settlers cannot be targeted or pushed and count as surviving animals. Before rolling, recruit at your edge habitat for free, with at most three travelling animals at once. If none are travelling and you remain in the game, recruit to take your turn. Starting food pays revival costs.

Optional opening mini-game

Choose Classic and enable Colonization opener in Settings. The first arrival chooses the first Classic player. If only one player survives, that player starts; if none survive, Player 1 starts. Then everyone begins fresh Classic with one animal, five food and four cards. The chosen order repeats each round.

Carrying capacity

Separate starting habitats spread animals out, but the shared destination supports a limited number of settlers. This illustrates dispersal and competition for finite habitat. If only one player remains, they win; if none remain, full Colonization ends without restarting.

Taking a turn

1 · Draw, choose and roll

At the start of your turn, draw one card if you hold fewer than four. Do not draw if you already have four. Cards are ready as soon as drawn. You may add or recruit an animal before rolling. Choose one animal and roll the movement die (1–6). You may switch animals before the first step; afterward your selection is locked.

2 · Move and stop

Move inward, outward or sideways between neighbouring blocks. Every step costs one movement point, including retracing your path. Square cells must share an edge; diagonal moves are unavailable. You may stop after one step without using the full roll. Passing through green or road spaces requires no check; stopping does. If no legal move is available, use Pass.

3 · Resolve and end

Resolve the landing check and any forced movement. Before rolling or before ending your turn, you may play Predator Block. After resolving your stop, click End turn. After everyone has acted, roll once to move all four officers; predators do not patrol.

Stealth and stationary predators

The Stealth Die

Every stop on a light green space requires one Stealth Check. Its six faces have these effects:

  • 1 predator face (number 1): place a predator on the animal’s block. The animal is hit unless it uses Play Dead. The predator stays after revival.
  • 2 safe faces (numbers 2–3): remain where you are; nothing happens.
  • 3 evade faces (numbers 4–6): choose an adjacent available block. No movement point is spent and no second Stealth Check is made. A road landing still requires the day/night die. Other existing landing effects, such as an officer escort, still apply.

Nearby predators: if a stationary predator occupies one of the eight squares surrounding the animal, the animal must make two additional Stealth checks after its first check if that first check does not catch it. A predator result on any required check catches the animal.

Blocked escape

You cannot voluntarily enter a predator’s block. If predators occupy every adjacent escape route when your Stealth Check resolves, the animal is automatically caught. If no empty evade space exists because other animals occupy the remaining routes, stay safely in place instead of starting another contest.

Building the predator population

No predators start on the board. A Stealth predator result adds one automatically; Predator Block adds one deliberately on any empty green block, anywhere on the board. Predators never roam. They block voluntary movement and cause a hit if an animal is pushed onto them.

There are 50 predator tokens. When all are deployed, click an existing predator to relocate it to the new destination. The total never exceeds 50. Deliberate placement and Play Dead relocation cannot place a predator on an animal, road, resource patch, habitat or refuge.

Roads, refuges and officers

Light gray road

Every time an animal stops on a road block, roll the day/night die: moon / odd = safe; sun / even = hit. Passing through requires no check. Every later road stop needs a fresh check, even if the previous one was safe. This also applies after a push, Move Enemy or an evade.

Median and protected refuges

The dark gray median needs no traffic check. Move along it in either direction or across it into a neighbouring lane. Two shield-marked refuges cannot be entered by officers. Animals cannot share them, and opponents may contest them.

Four traffic officers

After all players finish their turns, one shared roll moves all four officers clockwise. They skip refuges; only ordinary median blocks count as steps. Stopping on an officer, or having one stop on your animal, sends it home. Carried fruit returns to its original patch; banked food is unchanged and no revival cost is paid.

Hits, revival and elimination

Pay or rescue

A predator, car, hunter or off-board hit returns carried fruit to its original patch. Pay 2 banked food to revive at home. Carried fruit cannot pay the cost. An eligible rescue card may be used first. Play Dead keeps the animal and fruit in place while you move the predator to a different adjacent empty green block. Conservation Program revives for free after a car or hunter hit; its fruit still returns to the patch.

Keep playing while an animal survives

If you cannot pay, remove only the hit animal. You retain your cards and keep playing while another animal survives, including a settler. With none remaining, you are eliminated and your cards return to the deck. A hit or officer escort ends the affected animal’s unused movement. Home means the central habitat in Classic and your edge habitat in Colonization. Animals may share home safely; home animals and settlers cannot be targeted or contested.

Competition, theft and pursuit

Contest a block

Move onto an opponent’s animal. The challenger chooses odd or even; the defender receives the opposite. Roll once. Before accepting the result, the losing player may use an eligible competition reroll card, even outside their turn. Accept the final result to continue: the winner keeps the block and chooses an adjacent destination for the loser. Road checks, predator hits and officer escorts apply immediately. An edge contest in Classic may push the loser off the board.

Choose whether to steal

In Classic, a winner carrying no fruit may take the loser’s entire load before pushing it, or leave the load. Banked food cannot be stolen. Stolen fruit counts only after reaching home; a hit returns it to its original patch. Losing a load does not allow another harvest on the same trip, but it can be won back in a later contest.

Keep unused movement

After the push and landing effects, the challenger keeps unused movement whether it won or lost, unless a hit or officer escort ended it. Only the challenger continues; a defending winner waits for its own turn. The same two animals cannot contest again until next round. A pushed animal checks the road immediately; a moving challenger checks only when it stops.

Example

Rat rolls 5 and spends one step challenging Squirrel. After the contest, Rat has 4 steps left whether it wins or loses, provided the push does not cause a hit or officer escort. If Rat wins and steals food, it can use those steps to escape.

Cards · 53 in the deck

Start with four cards. Draw at most one replacement at the start of each turn, only while below four. Never replenish immediately after playing. You may play any number of eligible cards; rescue and competition-response cards can be used outside your turn when their conditions apply. There are no extra card draws for contests, traffic or hunters. When the deck empties, shuffle discarded cards into a new draw pile. Click the card icon beside a player’s name, or the deck, to view cards and use an eligible one.

Reroll ×14

Replace your movement roll before your first step.

Extra Block ×10

Add one step to your current movement.

Move Enemy ×6

Move an enemy to an adjacent empty block in any direction. Resolve landing effects; home animals and settlers are protected.

Safe Streets ×4

Skip one traffic check. Later road stops still require checks.

Play Dead ×2

After a predator hit, stay in place with your fruit. Place that predator on a different adjacent empty green block. Unavailable if none exists.

Conservation Program ×2

After a car or hunter hit, revive at home free. Carried fruit returns to its original patch.

Predator Block ×5

Before rolling or before ending your turn, choose an opponent and place a predator on an adjacent empty green block.

Interspecific Re-engagement ×6

After losing a competition roll, reroll before the push. Either contestant can respond when they lose.

Desperate Struggle ×2

After losing a competition roll, spend 1 banked food and reroll before the push. Odd/even contests have no ties.

Quick Sprint ×2

Add two steps to your current movement.

Board controls

Drag sideways to turn and vertically to tilt; pinch or wheel to zoom the board. 3D View offers separate turn, tilt and Z rotation. Move Board repositions it. Settings starts a fresh game; Classic and Colonization both use the square board layout.
