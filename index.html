How to play

Bring food home. Start with 3 food. Visit the fruit patch for 2 more, then return to the forest to bank it. First to 7 wins.

Roll, choose, move. One die per family. Move one animal up, down, left or right along gold squares, then finish your turn. An offspring costs 2 food; buy before rolling while an animal is home.

Watch the road. Stopping on light gray means a traffic roll: odd is safe, even is a hit. Passing through is safe; the dark strip needs no check.

Watch the predators. After all players, roll for each fox or tiger. They stay in their green lane and bounce at its edges. Only their final square catches you. You cannot step onto a predator.

Compete for a square. Enter an enemy's square, choose odd or even and roll. The winner pushes the loser one square, then clicks the deck for a card. Forced road landings need traffic checks; a predator or OUT push causes a hit.

A hit costs 1 food. Lose carried fruit and return home. With no food to revive, that animal is removed.

Your cards are beside your name. Click the card icon to view them, Use to play, or Close to keep them. New cards unlock next round.
