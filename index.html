How to play

Guide your animals through a landscape divided by roads. Choose routes, manage food and cards, and compete for space while avoiding predators and traffic.

01 · THE MAIN GAME

Habitat Fragmentation

Central habitat→Edge food→Home

Your animals leave the central habitat to collect food from resource patches at the edge. Collected fruit must return to the habitat before it is banked.

Gathering food

An ordinary patch provides 1 fruit. A golden horn patch provides 3 fruit, but requires a hunter check: moon means keep the load; sun means a hit. Each animal can harvest once per trip and carry one load. Patches stay empty after collection unless their fruit is returned following a hit.

Adding an animal · optional

Before rolling, spend 2 banked food to place another animal at home. Your existing animals can be anywhere. Keep at most three living animals; move only one each turn.

Winning Classic

  • Reach the banked-food target first: 10 by default, adjustable in Settings.
  • Be the only player with animals remaining. If this happens during a patrol, finish the patrol before declaring the winner.
  • If every resource patch is empty, compare banked food plus fruit carried by surviving animals after pending checks and rewards. Tied totals share the win.

Local extinction

If every animal is eliminated, nobody wins Classic. The board switches to full Habitat Colonization. Everyone receives one new animal, five food and two new cards; old scores and cards reset.

Board shapes

Choose Octagon, Circle or Square before starting. Square cells connect along shared edges; diagonal moves are unavailable. Its 24 food patches are spaced around the edge, with empty green cells between them. Outer square lanes contain more cells, so their patrol routes are longer.

02 · SETTLEMENT & THE OPTIONAL OPENER

Habitat Colonization

Edge habitats→Shared central habitat

Animals travel from separate edge habitats into the central habitat. Each arrival remains as a settler. There is no return journey and no food collection, hunters or golden harvests.

Full Colonization

The first player to establish 6 settlers wins. If the central habitat fills first, the player with the most settlers wins; ties share the victory. Its carrying capacity is 12 animals by default and can be changed in Settings.

Settlers can share the central habitat. They cannot be pushed or targeted by cards, and count as surviving animals. If only one player remains, that player wins; if none remain, Colonization ends without a winner.

Recruitment · optional

Before rolling, recruit another animal at your edge habitat for free. Keep at most three travelling animals at once; settlers do not count toward this limit. Starting food pays revival costs. If no travelling animal remains, recruit one to take your turn.

Mini-game: choose the first Classic player

In Settings, choose Classic and enable Colonization opener. The first animal to reach the central habitat earns its player the first turn in Classic. If only one player survives before an arrival, that player starts; if nobody survives, Player 1 starts.

The board then begins a fresh Classic game with the selected shape. Each player gets one animal, five food and two new cards. The chosen player acts first each round, followed by the other players in order. Nothing collected or spent during the opener carries over.

What carrying capacity represents

The destination has limited space for settlement. Separate starting habitats spread animals out initially, but all populations seek the same finite habitat. This is a simplified illustration of dispersal, survival and competition.

Shared rules · taking a turn

  1. ChooseSelect one of your travelling animals. Recruit first if you want another animal.
  2. RollRoll one movement die, from 1 to 6. You can switch animals before the first step; afterward the selection is locked.
  3. MoveEach neighbouring block costs one step. Move inward, outward or sideways, and retrace your path if you want. Returning over a block costs another step.
  4. StopYou may stop after one step without using the entire roll. Resolve any landing check. If no legal move is available, use Pass.
  5. PatrolAfter all players have acted, move the predators, then roll once for all four officers. The next round begins after these effects are resolved.

Read the landscape

Light gray roads

Every time an animal stops on a road block, roll the day/night die.

☾ Moon / oddSafe; stay on the block.☀ Sun / evenCar hit; follow revival rules.

Passing through without stopping needs no check. Stopping on a road again later requires a new roll, even after an earlier safe result.

Median & protected refuges

The dark gray median needs no traffic check. Move along it either way or cross into a neighbouring lane.

Two shield-marked refuge blocks align with the sides without golden harvests. Officers cannot enter them, but opponents can still contest them. Animals cannot share a refuge.

Four traffic officers

After the predators move, one shared die roll moves all four officers clockwise. They skip refuges; only ordinary median blocks count as steps.

If you stop on an officer, or one stops on your block, your animal returns home. Carried fruit returns to its original patch. Banked food is unchanged; no revival cost is paid.

Predator lanes

Three inner and three outer green lanes are patrolled by predators. Player animals have numbered flags; predators do not.

Predators move clockwise after everyone’s turn and hit animals only on their final blocks. You cannot voluntarily enter a predator’s block, but a push can place you there.

Settings allows 1–6 predators per lane, four by default, with a shared roll, one roll per lane, or individual rolls. Click Move all predators once; the sequence pauses for rescue decisions. Individual rolls can bring predators onto the same block.

Hits, revival & elimination

A predator, car, hunter or off-board hit returns carried fruit to its original patch. Pay 2 banked food to revive at home.

If you cannot pay, only that animal is removed. You keep playing and keep your cards while another animal survives, including a settler. When none remain, you are eliminated and your cards return to the deck.

A suitable rescue card can be used before paying. A hit or officer escort ends the affected challenger’s movement; a normal contest push into the habitat does not.

Habitat & food totals

Animals can share their home habitat. Safe home animals cannot be contested or targeted with Move Enemy. Settlers share the Colonization destination safely.

Banked food belongs to the player. Carried fruit belongs to the animal’s current load and is vulnerable during the journey. Carried fruit cannot pay a revival cost.

Competition, theft & the chase

1 · Contest a block

Move onto an opponent’s animal. The challenger chooses odd or even; the defender receives the opposite. Roll once. The winner keeps the block and chooses an adjacent block for the loser. You cannot contest your own animals.

Pushes trigger the destination’s effects immediately: a road check, predator hit, officer escort or other landing consequence. At a Classic outer-edge contest, the winner can also push the loser off the board.

2 · Choose whether to steal

In Classic, an empty-handed winner may choose Take fruit to take the loser’s entire load before pushing it, or Leave the fruit. An animal carrying a load cannot take another. Banked food cannot be stolen.

Stolen fruit must reach home to score. If its new carrier is hit, it returns to its original patch. An animal that loses a load cannot harvest again on that trip, but can win food back in another contest.

3 · Keep moving

After the push, forced landing checks and card rewards, the challenger keeps unused movement whether it won or lost. The winner can escape with food; the pushed loser can move around.

The same two animals cannot contest again until the next round, even if the defender initiates the rematch. A winner continuing across a road checks traffic only when it stops; a pushed animal checks immediately upon landing there.

Example

Rat rolls 5 and uses one step to challenge Squirrel, which carries 3 fruit. Rat wins, takes the load and pushes Squirrel aside. After the reward draw, Rat still has 4 steps to escape. If Rat loses instead, it still has 4 steps to move from its pushed position. Neither animal can challenge the other again until the next round.

Cards · 38 in the deck

Your two starting cards are ready in Round 1 when their conditions apply. Winning a contest or surviving a traffic or hunter roll earns one card: click the floating deck when prompted. Cards earned during play unlock next round. Using Safe Streets or a rescue card does not earn a survival reward.

Click the card icon beside a player’s name to view their hand. Use an eligible card or close the panel.

Reroll × 14

Replace your movement roll before taking your first step.

Extra Block × 10

Add one step to your current movement.

Move Enemy × 6

Choose an enemy, then move it one adjacent empty block in any direction. Landing effects apply. Home animals and settlers cannot be targeted.

Safe Streets × 4

Skip one traffic check and stay safely on that road block. Later stops still require checks.

Play Dead × 2

Survive a predator in place, keeping your fruit. Only the attacking predator rolls and moves again.

Angel Conservationists × 2

After a car or hunter hit, revive at home for free. Carried fruit still returns to its original patch.

Board controls

Drag sideways to turn and vertically to tilt. Pinch or wheel to zoom the board. 3D View adjusts turn, tilt and Z rotation separately. Move Board lets you reposition the board; Reset view restores the camera.

Settings starts a fresh game with your selected options. Reset restarts the chosen game; an enabled Colonization opener runs again.
