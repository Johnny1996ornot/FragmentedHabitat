How to play

Movement. Roll once per family and choose one animal. Move up to the die value in four directions. Predator squares are blocked. Choose a habitat starting row before stepping onto the field. Offspring cost 2 banked food at home; maximum 3 live animals.

Food. Collect 2 carried food at the resource patch once per trip and bank it at habitat. First to the selected banked-food target wins. Habitat and resource patches are shared regions; field squares hold one animal.

Predators. All five begin below the board in green field lanes, facing upward. No predator travels on the road. After all families play, each rolls and enters its fixed lane. Once inside, it reverses at the board edge. Only the final square hits. Cars remain outside the board as traffic markers.

Traffic. Stop on light gray and roll: even is hit, odd is safe. Crossing without stopping needs no check. Dark gray is a safe haven. Safe Streets may skip one traffic check before rolling.

Contests. A rival’s square costs one movement step to contest. Attacker picks odd/even; defender gets the opposite. Roll once. Winner keeps/takes the square and pushes the loser one adjacent square. A road push checks traffic immediately; a predator push immediately hits.

Off-board rewards. The winner may choose a highlighted OUT square at the edge. The loser pays the usual 1-food revival cost and returns home, losing cargo; at zero food that animal is removed. The contest winner draws one card. Only a contest off-board push awards a card.

17-card deck. Reroll ×7: replace your movement die before taking a step. Extra block ×5: add one step to this turn. Enemy backward ×3: move an enemy one square toward habitat if unoccupied; hazards still apply. Safe Streets ×2: skip one road landing check.

Card use. Newly earned cards are locked until the next round (after every family and all predators have played). They stay in your hand until you choose to use them. Click the labeled Use button above the board when enabled; drawing a card never activates it automatically. Used cards are discarded. When the deck empties, shuffle the discard pile; held cards stay in hands. An extinct family discards its hand. If all cards are held, no draw is available. There are no reward squares.

Revival. Hits spend 1 shared banked food, lose that animal’s cargo, and send it home. With no food, remove that token. A family continues while another animal survives. All families gone means no winner.

Undo. Normal movement can be undone before stopping. Contests and card use clear earlier undo history. Dice results, hazards, and card use are final. Refreshing loses the current game.
